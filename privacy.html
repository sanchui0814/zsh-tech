Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Zhongsheng Xincai Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. (中晟鑫财科技（北京）有限公司, “we”, “us”, or “the Company”) handles personal information in connection with the website at zshtech.com (the “Website”).

1. Scope

The Company is developing casual mobile games and does not currently operate a released app. This policy covers the Website only. It does not describe in-app collection, because there is no released app and the Company does not currently collect personal information through an app.

The Company does not currently offer payments, user accounts, or advertising.

2. Information the Website collects

The Website does not use accounts, contact forms, analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising. Visiting the Website does not require you to submit your name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details.

If you email admin@zshtech.com, we receive the address you write from, your message, and any details you choose to include. We use that information only to read and reply to your message.

Server or host logs may be created by the hosting provider as part of delivering the Website. We do not use those logs to identify you or to build a profile.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs on the Website. We do not ask for payment information.

4. How long email is kept

If you email us, we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records, then delete it when it is no longer needed.

5. Children

The Website is a company information site. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

If we later add accounts, payments, analytics, or advertising, including advertising SDKs in a game, we will update this policy before those practices begin and change the effective date above.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: admin@zshtech.com
